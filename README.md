# Market MavenX™

*by matrixX Technologies*

A research dashboard for crypto and US stocks. It ranks opportunities from 0 to 100, explains every pick in plain
English, watches what you already own, and tracks which companies are really investing in AI. It runs on your own
computer and never places a trade.

## Download

Get the latest version from **Releases** (on the right of this page):

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `MarketMavenX-1.0.0-windows.zip` |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13+ | `MarketMavenX-1.0.0-mac.zip` |

Then follow **QUICKSTART.pdf** (inside the download): install, launch and a 5-minute tour.

## What it does

- **Explained picks:** top 10 crypto and stocks scored from momentum, volume, trend, buzz, upside room and strength
  vs the market, each with Why, Risk and a trade plan.
- **AI adoption radar:** companies ranked by evidence of real AI investment (AI hardware named in SEC filings,
  spending jumps, AI news) and coins by code activity, with every line linked to its source.
- **Sell watch:** your holdings (read-only from Kraken, or added by hand) checked every 5 minutes.
- **Alerts:** new listings and IPOs, strong picks, big moves, AI-hardware filings and a morning digest.
